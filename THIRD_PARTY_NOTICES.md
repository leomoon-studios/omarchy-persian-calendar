## Jalali.py

The Gregorian/Persian conversion functions in `Model.js` are JavaScript ports
of the algorithm in Jalali.py by Mohammad Javad Naderi, itself ported from the
JDF project by Reza Gholampanahi.

The upstream source describes the original code as free and open source and
states that it may not be sold. Those original terms continue to apply to the
ported conversion functions.

Source: <https://github.com/mjnaderi/Jalali.py/blob/master/jalali.py>
